# Task 3: Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

## Problem Statement
The objective of this task is to perform Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on the Netflix dataset to identify underlying trends, patterns, and insights. This involves generating summary statistics, analyzing content distribution, and visualizing data to understand Netflix's catalog expansion over time.

## Dataset Details
* **Dataset Name:** Netflix Dataset
* **Source:** Kaggle
* **Link:** https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/shivamb/netflix-shows
* **Features:** Contains details on TV shows and movies available on Netflix, including cast, director, rating, release year, and duration.

## Approach
The analysis was conducted in a Jupyter Notebook using Python (`pandas`, `matplotlib`, `seaborn`). The following EDA steps were executed:
* **Summary Statistics:** Generated statistical summaries to understand the distribution of categorical and numerical variables.
* **Trend Identification:** * Visualized the ratio of Movies to TV Shows to understand platform focus.
    * Analyzed the timeline of content added to the platform to identify growth periods.
    * Mapped out the top 10 countries producing the most content on the platform.
* **Correlation Analysis:** Analyzed the relationship between the original release year of the content and the year it was added to the Netflix platform.

## Results & Insights
* **Content Dominance:** Movies significantly outnumber TV shows on the platform.
* **Growth Trend:** There was an exponential surge in content additions starting around 2015, peaking recently.
* **Global Reach:** The United States and India are the leading producers of content available on the platform.
* **Visual Evidence:** All insights are supported by corresponding bar charts, line graphs, and heatmaps generated during the analysis.
